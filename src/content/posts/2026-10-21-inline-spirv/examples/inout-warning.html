DXC could change this

HLSL defines inout as copy-in/copy-out; DXC only skips the copy when the argument's type matches exactly. It already switched to copying once (#7127), which broke these atomics, and reverted a month later (#7223).

See what it compiles to, and if the raw output (-fcgl -Vd) shows no copy in and out, it's safe to wrap these SPIR-V atomics in your own helper functions with inout.

In Nabla we decided not to take the risk, and added [[vk::ext_reference]] inout (#5675) to our DXC fork, used by nsc, so our helpers ask for the pointer explicitly.
